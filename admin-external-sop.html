PURPOSE AND SCOPE / MANUFA DA IYAKA

ENGLISH

This SOP standardizes how authorized ALBUKHR administrators receive, review, assess, document, escalate and decide External Project applications. Submission does not guarantee approval, funding, listing, activation or continued participation.

HAUSA

Wannan SOP yana daidaita yadda Admin mai izini zai karɓi, bincika, tantance, rubuta sakamako, tura matsala ga masu iko, sannan ya yanke hukunci kan External Project. Gabatar da application ba ya tabbatar da amincewa, samun jari, listing, activation ko ci gaba da shiga tsarin.

GOVERNING PRINCIPLES / MUHIMMAN ƘA’IDOJI

ENGLISH

Truthfulness and completeness; evidence-based decisions; verified ownership and authority; clear risk and conflict disclosure; appropriate custody and fund segregation where required; strict Mainnet/Testnet separation; auditability; and separation of application approval from operational activation.

HAUSA

Gaskiya da cikar bayanai; yanke hukunci bisa shaida; tabbatar da mallaka da ikon gabatar da project; bayyana haɗari da rikice-rikicen moriya; tsara custody da ware kuɗi inda ya dace; raba Mainnet da Testnet; kiyaye tarihin audit; da bambance amincewar application da kunna project a aiki.

ROLES AND SEGREGATION / MATSAYI DA RABON IKO

ENGLISH

Only authorized administrators may use the relevant workspace. The inspected application-review code requires an authenticated admin and an MFA/AAL2 step when the admin configuration requires it. Due-diligence, risk and funding pages show role checks in their frontend, but their server-side RPC authorization must be independently confirmed before this SOP can claim equivalent enforcement.

HAUSA

Admin mai izini ne kawai zai yi amfani da shafin da ya dace. Code na application review yana buƙatar authenticated admin, tare da MFA/AAL2 idan tsarin admin ya wajabta. Shafukan due diligence, risk da funding suna nuna role checks a frontend, amma dole ne a tabbatar da RPC/server-side authorization daban kafin SOP ta ce suna da kariya iri ɗaya.

ENGLISH

Where practical, separate application review, due diligence, risk assessment, funding decision and final activation authorization. An administrator must not approve their own conflict without disclosure and escalation under the applicable governance rules.

HAUSA

Inda zai yiwu, a raba aikin application review, due diligence, risk assessment, funding decision da final activation authorization. Admin bai kamata ya yanke hukunci kan rikicin moriyarsa ba tare da bayyana shi da tura shi ga mai izini bisa ƙa’idojin governance ba.

HIGH-LEVEL LIFECYCLE / JERIN MANYAN MATAKAI

ENGLISH

Authenticated application → initial administrative review → decision (revision / reject / approve) → conversion to a draft project registry record where authorized → ownership verification and due diligence → risk assessment → funding structure assessment → custody/escrow controls where required → terms review → disclosure review → funding and technical readiness → server-authoritative final activation gate → ACTIVE only when all applicable gates pass.

HAUSA

Application mai ingantaccen shiga → binciken farko na Admin → hukunci (gyara / ƙin amincewa / amincewa) → conversion zuwa draft project registry idan an ba da izini → tabbatar da mallaka da due diligence → risk assessment → funding structure assessment → custody/escrow inda ya dace → duba terms → duba disclosure → funding da technical readiness → final activation gate na server → ACTIVE ne kawai idan duk sharuddan da suka dace sun cika.

ENGLISH

Observed admin pages include: admin-external-projects.html; admin-external-due-diligence.html; admin-external-risk-assesment.html (note the current filename spelling); and admin-external-funding.html. Terms, custody, disclosure and final activation workspaces were not confirmed as dedicated pages in the inspected admin repository.

HAUSA

Shafukan Admin da aka gani sun haɗa da: admin-external-projects.html; admin-external-due-diligence.html; admin-external-risk-assesment.html (a lura da rubutun sunan fayil na yanzu); da admin-external-funding.html. Ba a tabbatar da shafuka na musamman na terms, custody, disclosure da final activation a repository ɗin Admin da aka bincika ba.

INITIAL APPLICATION REVIEW / BINCIKEN APPLICATION NA FARKO

ENGLISH

Open the application from the External Projects queue. Compare project and business names, application/project codes, network, declared funding, industry, owner identity, contact details, project purpose, team records, logo and submitted documents. Treat declarations as declarations until corroborated by suitable evidence.

HAUSA

Buɗe application daga jerin External Projects. Kwatanta sunan project da kasuwanci, application/project codes, network, adadin funding da aka bayyana, industry, bayanan mai project, hanyoyin tuntuɓa, manufar project, team, logo da takardun da aka gabatar. Kada a ɗauki bayanin applicant a matsayin tabbatacce sai an samu shaidar da ta dace.

ENGLISH

Available review actions observed in the code are Start Review, Request Revision, Reject, Approve and Convert to a draft External Project registry record. Revision instructions and rejection reasons are required by the frontend; approval reason is optional in the UI. The server response must confirm success before the action is considered complete.

HAUSA

Ayyukan review da aka gani a code sun haɗa da Start Review, Request Revision, Reject, Approve da Convert zuwa draft External Project registry record. Frontend yana buƙatar umarnin gyara da dalilin ƙin amincewa; dalilin amincewa na zaɓi ne a UI. Sai server ya tabbatar da nasara kafin a ɗauki aikin a matsayin an kammala.

DECISION RULES / ƘA’IDODIN HUKUNCI

ENGLISH

Request Revision when material information or evidence is missing, inconsistent, unclear or not fit for review. State exactly what is missing, acceptable evidence formats if known, and the response required. Reject only with a clear, factual reason and only through the authorized workflow. Approve the application only when the applicable review criteria are satisfied; approval is not activation.

HAUSA

A nemi gyara idan muhimman bayanai ko shaidu sun ɓace, sun saɓa, ba su bayyana ba ko ba su isa a yi review ba. A bayyana abin da ya ɓace, nau’in shaidar da ake buƙata idan an sani, da abin da ake son applicant ya yi. A ƙi application ne kawai da hujja bayyananniya ta gaskiya kuma ta hanyar da aka ba da izini. A amince idan sharuddan review da suka dace sun cika; amincewa ba activation ba ce.

ENGLISH

Do not use a direct database status update, browser manipulation or an alternate network to bypass a review decision.

HAUSA

Kada a yi direct database status update, browser manipulation ko amfani da wani network domin tsallake hukuncin review.

DUE DILIGENCE / TANTANCE SAHIHI DA KASUWANCI

ENGLISH

Start a due-diligence case only through the designated workspace and when the application is eligible for that stage. Review owner identity, authority to act, business registration where applicable, business identity, physical/operating location, operating status, legal/regulatory dependencies, related-party conflicts and operational evidence.

HAUSA

A fara due-diligence case ta workspace da aka ware kuma sai application ta cancanci wannan mataki. A bincika ainihin mai project, ikon wakilta, rajistar kasuwanci inda ya dace, asalin kasuwancin, wurin kasuwanci/aiki, matsayin aiki, abubuwan dogaro da doka/ƙa’ida, rikice-rikicen moriya da shaidar gudanar da aiki.

ENGLISH

For each check, choose a state that matches the evidence. Use verified_system only for system-verifiable evidence; documentary for authentic documents examined; project_declaration for applicant statements; and forecast_assumption for projections or assumptions. Record the evidence reference and concise notes. Never mark a check verified merely because a file was uploaded.

HAUSA

Ga kowane check, zaɓi state da ya dace da shaidar. Yi amfani da verified_system idan tsarin zai iya tabbatar da hujjar; documentary idan an bincika takarda ingantacciya; project_declaration idan bayanin applicant ne; forecast_assumption idan hasashe ko projection ne. Rubuta reference na shaida da taƙaitaccen bayani. Kada a sa verified saboda kawai an loda fayil.

ENGLISH

Register evidence against the relevant check where possible. If evidence is missing, conflicting, unverifiable or suspicious, record the limitation and keep the case blocked or escalate according to authority. Complete the case only after reviewing every required check; completion does not activate the project.

HAUSA

A danganta shaidar da check ɗin da ya dace idan zai yiwu. Idan shaida ta ɓace, ta saɓa, ba a iya tabbatar da ita ko tana da alamun zargi, a rubuta wannan kuma a bar case a blocked ko a tura shi ga mai iko. A kammala case bayan an duba duk required checks; kammalawa ba ya kunna project.

RISK ASSESSMENT / TANTANCE HAƊARI

ENGLISH

Begin only after due diligence is READY as required by the workspace. Review business model, execution, financial/economic, liquidity, operational, ownership/governance, fraud/market integrity and legal/regulatory risks. Evaluate each dimension using evidence, not intuition alone.

HAUSA

A fara ne kawai bayan due diligence ya zama READY kamar yadda workspace ya tanada. A duba haɗarin tsarin kasuwanci, aiwatarwa, kuɗi/tattalin arziki, liquidity, gudanar da aiki, mallaka/governance, zamba/ingancin kasuwa da doka/ƙa’ida. A tantance kowane ɓangare bisa shaida, ba zato kawai ba.

ENGLISH

Record assessment state, evidence level/reference, mitigation status and mitigation notes. Use hard-blocking flags only for justified blockers under the approved methodology. A risk band such as low, moderate, high or critical is a classification, not a guarantee of safety or returns. Complete the assessment only after required dimensions and blocking conditions have been reviewed.

HAUSA

Rubuta assessment state, evidence level/reference, mitigation status da bayanin mitigation. Yi amfani da hard-blocking flag ne kawai idan akwai dalili bisa methodology da aka amince da ita. Risk band kamar low, moderate, high ko critical rarrabuwa ce, ba tabbacin tsaro ko riba ba. A kammala assessment bayan an duba required dimensions da duk blockers.

FUNDING STRUCTURE ASSESSMENT / TANTANCE TSARIN JARI

ENGLISH

Open the funding workspace for the application. Review the funding plan and itemized categories, verify arithmetic and supporting evidence, and compare the requested amount with the server-calculated capital requirement. Distinguish capital requirement, required liquidity and verified liquidity; they are not interchangeable.

HAUSA

Buɗe funding workspace na application. Duba funding plan da jerin kashe-kashe, tabbatar da lissafi da shaidun tallafi, sannan kwatanta adadin da aka nema da capital requirement da server ya lissafa. A bambance capital requirement, required liquidity da verified liquidity; ba abu ɗaya ba ne.

ENGLISH

Run the server assessment before recording a decision. Choose Approve, Request Revision or Reject and record a reason. Do not increase or reduce the approved amount without an evidence-based rationale. Funding approval does not prove that funds are held by ALBUKHR, does not establish escrow/custody and does not authorize staking or activation.

HAUSA

A gudanar da server assessment kafin rubuta hukunci. Zaɓi Approve, Request Revision ko Reject tare da dalili. Kada a ƙara ko rage adadin da aka amince da shi ba tare da hujja ba. Amincewar funding ba ta tabbatar da cewa ALBUKHR na riƙe kuɗin ba, ba ta kafa escrow/custody, kuma ba ta ba da izinin staking ko activation.

CUSTODY, TERMS AND DISCLOSURE / KULAWA DA KUƊI, SHARUƊƊA DA BAYYANAWA

ENGLISH

Where the project model or applicable rules require custody, escrow, segregation, controlled release or reconciliation, do not mark the gate ready until the designated authorized process has verified the arrangement. Do not claim ALBUKHR custody or verified liquidity without evidence.

HAUSA

Inda tsarin project ko ƙa’idoji suka buƙaci custody, escrow, ware kuɗi, sakin kuɗi bisa matakai ko reconciliation, kada a sa gate a READY sai tsarin da aka ware kuma mai izini ya tabbatar da tsarin. Kada a ce ALBUKHR na riƙe kuɗi ko an tabbatar da liquidity ba tare da shaida ba.

ENGLISH

Terms must define the applicable investment/staking model, asset, target, limits, duration, funding window, reward/return model, principal treatment, exit/withdrawal, fees and conditions where applicable. Disclosure must separate verified facts from project declarations, forecasts and assumptions, and show material risks and conflicts. Dedicated admin pages for these gates were not confirmed in the inspected repository; follow the currently approved workflow and escalate rather than improvising a status change.

HAUSA

Terms su bayyana tsarin investment/staking da ya dace, asset, target, iyakoki, duration, lokacin funding, reward/return model, yadda ake kula da babban jari, fita/withdrawal, kuɗaɗen caji da sharudda inda ya dace. Disclosure ya bambance tabbatattun bayanai da bayanan applicant, hasashe da assumptions, sannan ya nuna muhimman haɗari da rikice-rikicen moriya. Ba a tabbatar da shafukan Admin na musamman na waɗannan matakai a repository da aka bincika ba; a bi workflow da aka amince da shi kuma a escalate maimakon ƙirƙirar status change.

FINAL ACTIVATION / KUNNA PROJECT A AIKI

ENGLISH

External activation must be a server-authoritative decision. Before activation, verify applicable gates for identity/registry, approval, due diligence, risk, funding, custody where required, terms, disclosure, funding readiness and technical readiness. The final action must record actor, role, project, network, previous/new status, reason, timestamp and relevant readiness snapshot/version where supported.

HAUSA

Activation na External dole ya zama hukuncin da server ke iko da shi. Kafin activation, tabbatar da gates da suka dace na identity/registry, approval, due diligence, risk, funding, custody inda ya dace, terms, disclosure, funding readiness da technical readiness. Aikin ƙarshe ya kamata ya adana actor, role, project, network, tsohon/sabon status, dalili, lokaci da readiness snapshot/version inda tsarin ya tallafa.

ENGLISH

Never activate through a frontend-only toggle or direct status UPDATE. If any mandatory gate is blocked, missing, stale or inconsistent, stop and escalate. Approval, conversion, visibility and ACTIVE are separate concepts.

HAUSA

Kada a yi activation ta frontend toggle kaɗai ko direct status UPDATE. Idan wani mandatory gate yana blocked, ya ɓace, ya tsufa ko ya saɓa, a tsaya a escalate. Approval, conversion, visibility da ACTIVE abubuwa ne dabam.

AUDIT TRAIL AND NETWORK ISOLATION / TARIHIN AUDIT DA RABON NETWORK

ENGLISH

Record decisions, evidence references, revision requests, rejections, mitigations, conflicts, escalations and material changes in the authorized record system. Do not store secrets or unnecessary sensitive identity data in free-text notes. Use the correct network and never use Testnet evidence or financial state to validate Mainnet.

HAUSA

A rubuta hukunci, references na shaida, buƙatun gyara, ƙin amincewa, mitigation, conflicts, escalations da manyan canje-canje a tsarin records da aka ba da izini. Kada a saka sirri ko bayanan identity masu tsanani da ba su da bukata a notes. A yi amfani da network da ya dace; kada a yi amfani da shaidar Testnet ko bayanan kuɗinsa wajen tabbatar da Mainnet.

ESCALATION MATRIX / LOKACIN TURA MATSALA

ENGLISH

Escalate immediately for suspected fraud or impersonation, altered/fabricated documents, ownership disputes, undisclosed conflicts, unexplained funding discrepancies, custody uncertainty, legal/regulatory concerns, security errors, cross-network data, unexpected status transitions or any request to bypass a gate. Preserve the evidence and do not promise an outcome.

HAUSA

A tura matsala nan take idan akwai zargin zamba ko kwaikwayon identity, takardun bogi/da aka canza, rikicin mallaka, rikice-rikicen moriya da ba a bayyana ba, bambancin funding da ba a yi bayani ba, rashin tabbas kan custody, matsalar doka/ƙa’ida, security error, gaurayawar network, canjin status da ba a zata ba ko buƙatar tsallake gate. A adana shaida kuma kada a yi alkawarin sakamako.
